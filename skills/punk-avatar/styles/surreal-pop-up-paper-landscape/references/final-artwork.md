# 单张效果图模式

Create one standalone transformed artwork at `3:4` unless the user specifies another ratio.

Apply every rule in `../STYLE.md`. Place the person and flattened scene centered slightly low, occupying about 30%–40% of warm off-white paper with broad clean negative space. The result should feel like quiet premium surreal photography, a paper installation, and a pop-up book.

Do not include the source photo, comparison, split screen, diptych, grid, frame, caption, title, logo, watermark, or alternative image.
