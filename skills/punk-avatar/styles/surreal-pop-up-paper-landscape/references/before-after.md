# 前后对比图模式

Create one `3:2` side-by-side comparison unless the user specifies another ratio.

- Two equal panels with a narrow neutral divider.
- Left: preserve the source photo unchanged—no retouching, recoloring, labels, decorative frame, or crop that removes the person.
- Right: apply every rule in `../STYLE.md`. Keep the person at a comparable scale and placement so the transformation reads immediately; the transformed group should occupy about 30%–40% of the panel and sit slightly low.
- Add no `before`/`after` labels, arrows, captions, titles, logos, or watermarks.

Output exactly one combined comparison image, not separate images or alternatives.
