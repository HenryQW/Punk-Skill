## Recommendation Priority

- Pet: `凌乱蜡笔宠物肖像`, `拍立得纪念卡`, or `怪诞灵魂手绘`; use `像素头像` for an icon-like result.
- Person: `怪诞灵魂手绘`, `时尚速写观察页`, or `像素头像`.
- Object or unclear: `像素头像` first.
- Match explicit paper/acrylic or pop-up-paper directions to their dedicated styles.
