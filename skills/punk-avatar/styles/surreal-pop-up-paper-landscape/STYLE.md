# 立体人物扁平景色纸艺

Modes: `before-after` and `final-artwork`; the workflow selects one mode reference before compilation.

## Shared Visual Atom

- Use the uploaded photo as the only visual reference. Keep exactly one primary person as the only upright, sharp, photorealistic, fully three-dimensional subject.
- Preserve identity, face, hair, expression, clothes, accessories, action, gesture, body proportions, pose, camera angle, lighting, and color; never beautify, redesign, cartoonize, duplicate, or deform the person.
- Fold every non-person element backward around the feet/body contact point by `75°–85°`; compress its vertical height to `10%–20%`, with stronger depth compression in distant scenery.
- The person is perpendicular to the paper; the environment lies parallel to it. Spread the scene left, right, and rear as a wide shallow oval/fan joined naturally to the contact point.
- Retain recognizable scene elements, photographic texture, and color relationships while slightly reducing environmental saturation, sharpness, and contrast. Only the thinning outer edge may dissolve into restrained dry-brush, watercolor, or paper fibers.
- Use warm off-white fibrous art paper, generous negative space, and one short soft contact shadow.

## Never

Upright non-person scenery; ordinary background replacement or blur; full-scene watercolor; flat collage; shared person/environment plane; floating gaps; halos; hard cutout edges; long hard pointed shadows; extra people; changed anatomy; oversized head; text; frames; borders; logos; watermarks.
